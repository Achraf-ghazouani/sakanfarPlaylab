How it works

Paper to playable in four steps. No app, no account.

1
✏️
Draw

Any paper, any pen. A ground line, a stick figure where you start, a flag at the end. Then go wild.

2
📸
Snap

Take a photo. Muse Spark reads what you meant: a cactus is an enemy, a cloud is a moving platform.

3
🤖
Playtest

An agent simulates every jump. Impossible ones get fixed, with the AI adding steps or lowering a ledge.

4
🎮
Play

Right on your phone, with big touch buttons. Your drawing stays in the corner so everyone sees the magic.
