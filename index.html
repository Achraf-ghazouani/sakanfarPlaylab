How it works

  1. Say it

    A sentence, a 2D sketch, or a drawing held up to your phone camera. Muse Spark answers with a short plan you can change for free.

  2. Muse Wonder writes it

    The whole game as code, tested in a sandbox. It fixes its own bugs, then looks at the result.

  3. Play it

    On the keyboard, or with up to four phones as controllers. Publish a link, let friends remix it.
