IF YOU CAN DRAW IT,
YOU CAN PLAY IT.

Introducing Sakanfar PlayLab, an experimental game lab where a pen and a sheet of paper are the level editor. Sketch, snap, play. No coding, no symbols to learn.

Stop dreaming of levels and start drawing them. A ground line, a stick figure where you start, a flag at the end, then anything goes: a cactus becomes an enemy, a cloud becomes a moving platform, circles become coins. It is your paper, you make the rules.

Point your phone at the paper. Muse Spark reads what you meant, a playtest agent fixes every impossible jump, and the level builds itself on the big screen while you keep drawing.
